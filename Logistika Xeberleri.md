### Privacy Policy
#### Effective Date: May 17, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how our Meta Application ("the App") handles information when you use our services to manage Facebook Page posts and comments.

1. No Data Collection or Storage.
   Our App does not collect, harvest, or store any personal data. We do not operate databases to save your information, and we do not track your activity.
   
3. Meta Graph API Usage
   The App acts strictly as a tool to interact with the Meta Graph API. It accesses your Facebook Page data in real-time solely to execute your commands to: Create, edit, and delete posts on your managed Pages. Create, edit, and delete comments on your managed Pages.
   
5. Data Sharing
   Because we do not collect or store any data, we do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user information with third parties.
   
6. Data Security & Control
   All actions are processed through Meta’s secure official API. You maintain total control over your data. You can revoke the App's access at any time through your Facebook account settings under "Apps and Websites."
