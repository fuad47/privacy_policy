Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2025

Last Updated: September 22, 2025

Quick Summary: Mektub stores your profile data locally on your device only. We don't collect, store, or sell your personal information. Your email content is transmitted securely to send emails to government organizations.

1. Information We Collect and Store

1.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Mektub stores the following information only on your device using local browser storage:

  • Profile Information: Your name, surname, email address, and FIN code (optional)
  • Usage Data: Daily email count and last usage date for rate limiting
  • Draft Data: Temporary storage of email recipients, subject, and message while composing
  • Sent Email History: Local record of emails you've sent (for your reference only)

1.2 Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

The following data is considered personally identifiable:

  • Your full name (required)
  • Your email address (required)
  • Your FIN code (optional, if provided)
  • Email content you compose
  • File attachments you include

2. Data Transmission to Our Servers

2.1 Email Sending Data

When you send an email, the following data is transmitted to our servers:

  • Recipient email addresses (from our predefined government organization list)
  • Email subject and message content
  • Your name and email address (as sender information)
  • File attachments (if any)

2.2 Server Data Handling

Our servers:

  • Process emails immediately and send them via Resend email service
  • Do not store your email content, attachments, or personal information
  • Do not log email content beyond basic delivery confirmation
  • Use encryption for all data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)

3. How We Use Your Data

3.1 Primary Purpose

  • Email Delivery: To send your emails to selected government organizations
  • Rate Limiting: To enforce daily email limits (2 emails per day)
  • User Experience: To pre-fill forms and maintain your preferences

3.2 We Do NOT Use Your Data For

  • Marketing or advertising
  • Selling to third parties
  • Analytics or tracking
  • User profiling
  • Any purpose other than email delivery

4. Data Deletion and User Rights

4.1 Local Data Deletion

You have full control: All data stored locally can be deleted by:

  • Clearing your browser data/cache
  • Uninstalling the app
  • Using the "Clear Profile" feature in the app (if implemented)

4.2 Server Data

No deletion needed: We don't permanently store your personal data on our servers. Email content is processed and discarded immediately after sending.

4.3 Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your data in the app's profile section
  • Deletion: Clear local storage or contact us for assistance
  • Portability: Your data is stored locally and fully accessible to you

5. App Permissions

5.1 Required Permissions

  • Internet Access: Required to send emails to government organizations
  • Storage Access: Required to save your profile and preferences locally
  • File Access: Required to attach PDF documents and images to emails

5.2 Sensitive Permissions

  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Allows you to select PDF and image files from your device to attach to emails. Files are only accessed when you explicitly select them and are not stored permanently.
  • CAMERA (if applicable): May be used to capture images for email attachments (user-initiated only)

5.3 Why These Permissions Are Needed

  • File access enables you to attach important documents to your government correspondence
  • Storage access keeps your profile information for convenience across app sessions
  • Internet access is essential for the core email sending functionality

6. Security Measures

  • HTTPS Encryption: All data transmission is encrypted
  • Local Storage: Data remains on your device under your control
  • No Server Storage: Minimizes risk by not storing sensitive data remotely
  • File Validation: Attachments are validated for security (PDF/images only, 10MB limit)

7. Third-Party Services

We use Resend email service to deliver your emails. Resend processes your email content only for delivery purposes and does not store your personal information. Please refer to Resend's Privacy Policy for more information.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is intended for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.
